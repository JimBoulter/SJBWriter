# 3.4 — What's Next?

Now that you've completed the basic tutorial and written your first AI-assisted prose, I recommend taking a look at the **EPBM Intro** in the Prompts pane on the right. You'll find more tips on using the EPBM prompt set, organizing your writing files, and customizing prompts for your own projects.

And if you like the Dragon Egg story you started here, why not keep going? Copy the story context, create a new Project (using the ellipsis menu beside the Project dropdown), then create a Background file and paste that context into it. If you like some of the prose you've already written, copy that into a new Writing file, too, and continue the story!

The EPBM prompts will still be there in the Prompts pane. They won't disappear unless you specifically delete or replace them.

The **System** pane, just above Prompts, provides another way to give the AI general instructions that can accompany your writing prompts. Take a look at the examples I've included. You'll notice one named **Always On Instructions**, and it pretty much is! It contains instructions for writing in my usual style. But just as you can customize the EPBM prompts for a particular project, you can customize your System instructions, too. For my Seven Dials series, I created a different set of instructions to guide the AI toward Agatha Christie's writing style. I use those instead of *my* Always On Instructions when working on that series.

You've learned the important parts of SJB Writer. Now go out there and write!
