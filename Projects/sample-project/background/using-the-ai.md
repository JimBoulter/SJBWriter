# 3.1 - Using the AI

**Let's put AI to work!**

You've learned the basics. Now let's generate something.

## Step 1: Select Your Model

Look at the bottom of the Editor for the model dropdown. If you've already selected a model, its name will appear there. Otherwise, click **-- Select Model --** and choose one.

You can change models here anytime without opening Settings.

## Step 2: Give It an Instruction

Copy this prompt:

**Write an approximately 50-word poem about pineapple-eating dragons.**

Paste it into the **Instruction** box below the Editor.

## Step 3: Generate!

Click in the blank space below these instructions in the Editor. That's where your poem will appear.

Now click the blue **Generate** button below the Instruction box, or press **Command-Return (Mac) / Ctrl-Enter (Windows)** on your keyboard.

That's it! You've just used AI to help create something in SJB Writer.

And it's your writing now. Change a word, rewrite a line, or throw the whole thing away and try again. The AI is here to help you write, not tell you what to write.

When you're ready, open **3.2 - Prompting** to learn how to get more control over what the AI writes.
