# Welcome to SJB Writer

**Your stories. Your worlds. Your words.**

Every story begins somewhere. Maybe you have a character who won't leave you alone, a world waiting to be explored, or a plot that's been rattling around in your head for years.

SJB Writer is your workspace for bringing those ideas together, organizing your projects, and turning them into stories. Make it your own, work at your own pace, and keep your attention where it belongs: on the writing.

## Before You Begin

SJB Writer keeps things organized into a few simple areas:

- **Projects** keep your stories and their materials separate.
- **Background** holds character profiles, worldbuilding, research, and other reference material.
- **Writing** holds your manuscript and other writing files.
- **Prompts** tell your chosen AI model what you'd like it to do.

You don't need to set everything up right now. We'll walk through the basics and get you ready for your first AI generation.

## Let's Get Started
