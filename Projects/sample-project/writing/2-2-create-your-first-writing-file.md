# 2.2 - Create Your First Writing File

**Let's write!**

Let's create a file of your own.

1. In the **Writing** pane, move your mouse over the **2 - Getting Started** folder.
2. Click the **three dots (⋯)** that appear beside it, then select **Add File**.
3. Give your file a name. Anything you like! Save it, and your new file will appear in that folder.
4. Select your new file, click in the Editor, and start typing.

Write anything. A sentence, a paragraph, or the opening of your next great novel. Shouldn't it be that easy?

## What About Saving?

Never losing a word is important to me. SJB Writer is **local-first**, meaning your writing is saved on your own computer, not on some remote service.

Your work saves automatically, including when you switch files. There's also a little floppy disk icon near the bottom-right of the Editor if you want to save manually. (Yes, that's what we used to save things on!)

Try it: Type a sentence in your new file, switch to another file, then switch back. Your words should be right where you left them.

## A Quick Look at Folders

Files can live directly in the Writing pane or inside folders, like the one you just used.

Want to try creating a folder? Click the **folder icon** beside the Writing pane heading, give it a name, and save it.

You can organize your writing into folders and subfolders, and move files around as your project grows. There's no need to organize everything perfectly today.
