# 2.1 - Look Around

**Getting comfortable with your workspace**

The large middle area of SJB Writer is the **Editor**. This is where you write, edit, and work with AI-generated text. Everything around it helps you organize your work and control the AI.

## Your Projects

On the left, you'll see **Sample Project** at the top. Click the arrow beside its name to switch between projects. As you create more projects, they'll appear in that list.

Below the project name are two panes:

- **Background** is for outlines, character profiles, worldbuilding, research, and anything else you want to keep handy while writing.
- **Writing** is for your actual writing files. Organize them into folders however you like, whether by chapter, draft, or anything that suits your workflow.

You've already used both panes to get here!

## AI Setup

On the right, you'll find **System** and **Prompts** under AI Setup. These control how your chosen AI model works with your writing. We'll explore them soon. For now, just know where they are.

## Make Yourself Comfortable

You can drag the inner edges of the side panels to resize them. You can also collapse the panels using the icons above them to give the Editor more room.

I designed SJB Writer to keep distractions out of your way, so feel free to hide the panels whenever you want to focus on your writing. I happen to agree!
