---
title: "EPBM 1 - Premise"
---

Using the genre and any additional direction provided in the Instruction field or selected context, develop five distinct, original premises for a novel.

For each premise, include:

- A working title
- The setting
- The central character or characters
- The core conflict
- A brief description of what makes the story compelling

Make the five premises meaningfully different in their characters, conflicts, or approaches to the genre. Keep each concise enough to compare easily.

Do not develop characters or an outline yet. The author will select and refine a premise before continuing.
