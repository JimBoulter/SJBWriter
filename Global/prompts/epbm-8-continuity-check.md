---
title: "EPBM 8 - Continuity Check"
---

Review the written chapters against the established premise, characters, outline, and previous chapters.

Identify all deviations, even if they are narratively effective.

Distinguish between:
1. **Continuity errors:** Contradictions in established facts, events, characterization, or chronology.
2. **Outline deviations:** Events, decisions, or developments that differ from the outline but may be intentional improvements.

For each finding, identify the relevant chapter, explain the discrepancy, and recommend whether to revise the manuscript or consider updating the outline.

Do not rewrite the story. Do not flag previously accepted changes as new errors.
