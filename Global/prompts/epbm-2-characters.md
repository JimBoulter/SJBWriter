---
title: "EPBM 2 - Characters"
---

Using the selected premise and any additional context, develop 3–5 main characters who will drive the story.

For each character, include:

- Name, age, and role in the story
- Personality and defining traits
- Relevant background and formative experiences
- Primary goals, motivations, and internal conflicts
- Strengths, flaws, and vulnerabilities
- Important relationships or potential conflicts with other characters
- A proposed character arc, including how the character might change over the course of the story

Ensure the characters are distinct, believable within the story's genre and setting, and capable of creating meaningful conflict.

Let the premise guide their development, but leave room for their arcs and relationships to evolve during outlining.

Do not begin the outline. Present the character profiles for the author's review and refinement.
