# Example: Customizing EPBM for a Series

This is an example of how I modified one of the original EPBM prompts for a specific writing project. I created and saved this prompt, along with several others, to use while writing my Seven Dials series.

The prompt references a project-specific Background file, *Seven Dials Fan Fiction Primer*, which establishes the characters, setting, writing conventions, and other details I want maintained throughout the series.

You can do the same with your own projects. Create a Background file containing your story's established information, customize the instructions for your genre and writing style, and save the modified prompt for repeated use.

## Seven Dials — Chapter Plan

Using the established outline, relevant previous chapters, and the Seven Dials Fan Fiction Primer, create a detailed writing plan for the next chapter.

Treat the Seven Dials Fan Fiction Primer as the authoritative reference for established series canon, character identities, relationships, setting, and writing conventions. Maintain consistency with previous chapters and the established Agatha Christie-style murder mystery genre.

[chapter X plan]

- **Scene Breakdown:** Plan the sequence of scenes, their settings, purposes, and key actions.
- **Character Interactions:** Identify who appears, their motivations, and how their relationships or suspicions develop.
- **Key Plot Advancements:** Identify clues, discoveries, complications, and decisions that advance the mystery within this chapter.
- **Pacing and Structure:** Plan sufficient meaningful material to support at least 4,500 words of narrative prose.
- **Opening Hook:** Begin with a compelling development that draws the reader into the chapter.
- **Chapter Ending:** End with a cliffhanger that creates anticipation without revealing events reserved for later chapters.

[/chapter X plan]

Before completing the plan, verify that it:

- Aligns with the Seven Dials Fan Fiction Primer and established murder mystery genre.
- Advances the existing central mystery without redefining it.
- Maintains consistency with previously written chapters and established character development.
- Introduces clues and revelations at the appropriate points in the outline.
- Does not prematurely reveal future events, solutions, or character knowledge.
- Ends at the intended boundary of the requested chapter.

Present only the chapter plan, ready for review before drafting.
