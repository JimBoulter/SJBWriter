CONTINUITY LOCKDOWN RULES

If information appears only in a later chapter outline and has not yet occurred in the story, act as though it does not exist.
The current chapter must be planned solely from:
    - The locked Premise and Characters file.
    - Previously completed chapters.
    - The specific outline entry for the requested chapter.
Never merge content from multiple outline chapters.
Never advance the story beyond the ending point of the requested chapter.
Stop planning exactly where the requested chapter ends.
