---
title: "EPBM 3 - Outline"
---

Using the selected premise, character profiles, and any additional context, develop a detailed, chapter-by-chapter outline for the novel.

Follow any provided requirements for chapter count, length, structure, genre, and pacing. If no chapter count is specified, propose an appropriate structure for a full-length novel.

Begin with a brief overview of the story's major acts or narrative phases, identifying the central conflict, major turning points, climax, and resolution.

For each chapter, include:

- Chapter number and working title
- Primary plot events and significant developments
- Character motivations, decisions, and developments
- Conflicts, complications, or revelations that move the story forward
- How the chapter advances the overall narrative and leads into the next

Ensure that:

- The central conflict develops logically, with meaningful escalation and consequences.
- Character arcs progress naturally through their actions and decisions.
- Subplots support the main story and reach appropriate resolutions.
- Major revelations are properly established before their payoff.
- The ending resolves the principal story conflicts in a manner consistent with the premise and genre.

Maintain consistency with the established premise and characters. Do not introduce major changes to either without identifying them for the author's consideration.

Do not begin chapter planning or prose drafting. Present the complete outline for the author's review and refinement.
