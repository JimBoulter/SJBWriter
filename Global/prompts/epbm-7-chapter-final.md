---
title: "EPBM 7 - Chapter Final"
---

Using the current chapter draft and its editing plan, produce a polished Final Draft of the requested chapter.

Apply the editing recommendations where they improve the writing, while preserving effective passages, the author's established voice, and the chapter's intended events and structure.

[chapter X final]

- Write at least 1,500 words of complete narrative prose.
- Address the substantive issues identified in the Edit Plan, including clarity, characterization, dialogue, pacing, and continuity.
- Refine awkward, repetitive, clichéd, or generic phrasing without introducing unnecessary changes.
- Preserve effective dialogue, descriptions, and narrative passages rather than rewriting them simply for variety.
- Maintain consistency with the established premise, characters, previous chapters, and approved story developments.
- Respect the chapter's intended boundaries. Do not introduce events or revelations reserved for later chapters.
- Maintain the established point of view, tense, tone, and narrative style.
- Preserve the chapter's opening hook and ending impact, improving them only where needed.

[/chapter X final]

Produce only the complete, polished chapter prose. Do not include editing notes, explanations, summaries, or commentary.
