title: "EPBM 6 - Chapter Editing Plan"
---

Review the prose draft of the requested chapter and create a focused, actionable editing plan to improve its quality while preserving the author's intent, established voice, and story continuity.

Evaluate the actual chapter draft, whether it appears in the current Editor or selected Context. Use the chapter plan and other story materials as reference, but do not critique them as though they were the prose draft.

[chapter X editing plan]

### 1. Areas for Improvement

Identify specific weaknesses in scene development, dialogue, description, character interactions, emotional impact, and narrative clarity.

Recommend changes that strengthen the chapter without introducing unnecessary events or altering its intended direction.

### 2. Consistency Checks

Evaluate tone, point of view, characterization, setting, and continuity with established events.

Identify contradictions or unintended departures from the chapter plan, distinguishing genuine continuity problems from reasonable creative developments.

Do not introduce information or events from future chapters.

### 3. Style and Pacing Refinements

- Identify cliché or overly familiar phrasing and recommend plain, specific, plot-functional alternatives.
- Replace abstract emotional labels with observable behavior, sensory detail, or concrete internal reactions where appropriate.
- Strengthen intensity through pacing, proximity, and escalating stakes rather than stronger adjectives or repetition.
- Vary sentence rhythm and paragraph structure where patterns become repetitive.
- Remove explanations that restate what the scene already implies.
- Identify unnecessary softening constructions that weaken otherwise clear statements.
- Condense repeated emotional or informational beats.
- Flag generic AI phrasing and suggest grounded, situation-specific alternatives.

### 4. Revision Priorities

Organize the recommended changes by importance, identifying which revisions will most improve the chapter.

Preserve passages that already work well. Do not recommend changes simply for the sake of editing.

Produce only the editing plan, not rewritten chapter prose or a chapter summary. Make recommendations specific enough to guide the Final Draft revision.
